# Restaurant Sales & Operations Analytics Dataset

## Overview
This dataset contains comprehensive sales, operations, marketing, and advertising data for a restaurant chain over an 18-month period (January 2023 - June 2024). The data is organized across multiple sheets with detailed metrics that enable analysis of business performance across different dimensions.

## Dataset Structure

### 1. Sales Data
- **Time Period**: Monthly data from Jan 2023 - Jun 2024
- **Key Metrics**:
  - Order volumes by type (Classic, UberEats, Pickup, Subscription, etc.)
  - Sales revenue
  - Average order value (AOV)
  - New vs. repeat customers
  - Customer frequency
  - Year-over-year growth metrics
- **Insights**: Contains 11 pre-identified insights about order patterns, customer behavior, and revenue trends

### 2. Operations Data
- **Key Metrics**:
  - Delivery time components (Assignment, D2R, ADW, R2C)
  - Order accuracy (M+I rate)
  - Store uptime
  - Merchant ratings
  - Comparison with peer benchmarks
- **Insights**: 7 operational insights about performance vs. competitors and trends

### 3. Marketing Data
- **Campaign Details**: 16 historical marketing campaigns
- **Key Metrics**:
  - Campaign duration and reach
  - Sales impact
  - New customer acquisition
  - Return on ad spend (ROAS)
  - Cost metrics (CPA, CAC)
- **Insights**: 4 insights about campaign effectiveness and efficiency

### 4. Advertising Data
- **Campaign Details**: 6 ad campaigns with different targeting approaches
- **Key Metrics**:
  - Impressions and clicks
  - Conversion rates
  - Customer acquisition costs
  - Return on investment
- **Insights**: 7 insights about ad performance and optimization opportunities

## Case Study Objectives

1. **Performance Analysis**:
   - Identify trends in sales and order types
   - Analyze customer retention and repeat behavior
   - Evaluate operational efficiency

2. **Marketing Effectiveness**:
   - Compare performance across different campaign types
   - Determine most effective customer acquisition strategies
   - Optimize marketing spend allocation

3. **Operational Improvements**:
   - Identify bottlenecks in delivery processes
   - Benchmark performance against industry peers
   - Improve order accuracy and uptime

4. **Strategic Recommendations**:
   - Develop data-driven strategies for growth
   - Optimize pricing and promotions
   - Improve customer lifetime value
